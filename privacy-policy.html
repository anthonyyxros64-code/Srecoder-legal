Privacy Policy

App: Srecoder (Android screen recorder)
Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Srecoder handles information when you use the app.

Information the app accesses

  • Screen content: When you choose to start a screen recording or screenshot, Android displays its system screen-capture consent prompt. Srecoder captures the screen only after you grant that permission.
  • Microphone audio: If you enable microphone recording and grant Android's microphone permission, audio is captured as part of your recording.
  • Camera: If you choose the optional Facecam feature and grant camera permission, the app displays a camera preview.
  • Media files: The app accesses videos or images you select or create for playback, editing, exporting, and local organisation.

How information is used and stored

Screen recordings, screenshots, microphone audio, camera preview, and media editing are used to provide the features you request. The app is currently designed to process and store its recordings and media locally on your device. It does not operate an account system or upload recordings to an app-operated server.

The current source configuration does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before release.

Sharing

Srecoder does not send your recordings or captured screen content to the developer. If you choose Android's share or export actions, the selected file is shared with the app or destination you choose through Android's sharing features. The receiving app or service handles that file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions and your choices

You control whether to grant screen-capture, microphone, camera, notification, and display-over-other-apps access. You can deny or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Screen capture requires Android's system consent flow when initiated.

Data retention and deletion

Media files saved by the app remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Files exported to shared device storage may remain there after uninstall and must be deleted by you using your device's file manager or gallery app. The app does not maintain a remote copy.

Children

Srecoder is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security

Because media is processed locally, keep your device protected with its screen lock and use Android's built-in file and app permissions. No method of local storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
